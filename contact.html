Before you write

Might already be answered

How do I join?

Join the general body listserv any time; committee applications open in fall recruitment and whenever a seat opens up. Full details on the Get Involved page.

Are your events open to non-members?

Most professional and educational events are open to all Penn students. Social events are for general body and committee members. See what's coming up.

Can my firm recruit through WUEG?

Yes — that is half of our stated mission. Email the Careers address above and we will send the current sponsorship document. What's on offer.

I'm an alum. How do I get back on the list?

Email the general address with your graduation year and where you work now, and we will add you to the alumni database. More ways to help.

I have a concern about how the club handled something.

Raise it with any officer, or with the President directly. Our constitution sets out written process and appeal rights for membership and officer decisions. Concerns involving harassment, hazing, or discrimination are reported to Penn's Office of Student Affairs and, where applicable, the Wharton Council and the Center for Community Standards and Accountability — and you can contact those offices directly at any point.
