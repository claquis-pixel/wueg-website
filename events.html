Every year

Signature programming

Specific dates move, but the shape of the year is stable. This is what you can count on as a member.

Energy Career Fair

Early fall. Sponsor and partner firms in one room, specifically for students interested in energy. Run by Careers with Events & Marketing.

General Body Meetings

Every other week to once a month. Club updates plus a short teaching session from a committee. The main way to stay connected without joining a committee.

Speaker Events & Panels

Professionals from across the value chain, plus regulators, policymakers, and public affairs professionals brought in by the Policy committee.

Info Sessions

Recruiting events where a sponsor or partner gives advice and information about their company and how to get hired there.

Case Competition

End of year. A live energy problem, judged by professionals, developed by Finance, Consulting, and Research together.

Socials & Mixers

Meet-and-greets and mixers run by Membership, including joint events with the Wharton MBA energy club. Social events may carry a $5 admission fee.
