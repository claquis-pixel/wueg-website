The WUEG Newsletter

One issue a month, covering a broad array of energy topics — emissions trading in Europe, the latest oil and gas M&A deal, sustainable start-ups, the politics surrounding the Texas electricity grid.

It is also how members hear about upcoming events and open applications first, so it is the one thing worth subscribing to even if you read nothing else here.

On balance

Policy work published by WUEG is nonpartisan and balanced when presenting an issue to the club. That is a standing requirement of the committee, written into our constitution, not a case-by-case judgment call.
