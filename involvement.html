About the attendance rule

"Without proper notice" is the important half of that sentence. Tell your VP or the VP of Membership that you have a conflict and the absence is excused. The rule exists so committees can plan around who is actually showing up, not to catch people out — and if your status does change, you can regain it at the start of the following semester, or earlier with your VP's approval.

Committee applications

How it works

Recruitment runs in the early fall, and off-cycle whenever a committee or board seat opens up.

  1. Step 1

    The opening is announced

    New openings go out through the club newsletter with the application attached. Applications stay open for a week to a week and a half.

  2. Step 2

    You apply

    A short written application. We are looking for genuine interest in the space and evidence that you follow through on what you take on — not a finance résumé.

  3. Step 3

    Interview

    Interviews happen within five days of the application closing. An interview always occurs — there is no path onto a committee or the board that skips it.

  4. Step 4

    Decision

    The VP of the relevant committee determines the final candidates, and the board votes. For board roles, the candidate with the most support in approval voting takes the seat.

  5. Then

    Onboarding

    For board transitions, incoming leaders typically join as Associate VPs a semester early and are onboarded by the person they are succeeding.

Before you ask

Questions people actually have

I'm a freshman with no energy background. Is that a problem?

No. It describes most of the people who join. The education sessions in Research, Policy, Consulting, and Finance exist because the sector is genuinely hard to pick up on your own.

I'm in the College or Engineering, not Wharton.

Also fine. All Penn students are able to join, and the mix is part of what makes the club work. Only one constitutional requirement touches school affiliation: at least one President must be a Wharton student.

How much time does a committee take?

Committees meet weekly, plus general body meetings every other week to monthly, plus whatever your assignments require. It varies by committee and by point in the semester — Consulting during an engagement and Events in the week before the career fair are the heavy ones. Ask the VP directly in your interview; they will tell you honestly.

Can I be in more than one committee?

Talk to the VPs involved. The constraint is whether you can meet the commitments of both, not a rule against it.

I missed fall recruitment. Do I wait a year?

No. Join the general body now and watch the newsletter — vacancies open throughout the year and are announced there with the application attached. Being a visible, active general body member is the best possible preparation for that.

Is there really no fee?

WUEG does not collect dues. The one exception written into our constitution is that social events may carry a $5 admission fee.
