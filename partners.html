Partners & recruiting

Reach Penn students who already know the sector

WUEG is the only undergraduate organization at Penn focused solely on energy. Our members have self-selected into the industry before you ever meet them.

Why WUEG

A pre-filtered audience

Energy recruiting at undergraduate level has a discovery problem in both directions. Firms across the value chain — developers, utilities, traders, infrastructure funds, energy-focused consulting and banking teams — compete for attention against a campus pipeline built around a handful of large, well-known employers. Students who are genuinely curious about power markets often never learn those roles exist.

WUEG closes that gap. Our members attend education sessions on regulation and project economics, write research on technologies and policy, and run client consulting engagements. By the time they reach your table, the conversation starts several steps in.

They also come from across the University — Wharton, Engineering, the College, and Nursing — so the pool is not limited to one skill profile.

What a partnership looks like

Our VP of Careers maintains the sponsorship document, keeps it current each year, and makes sure commitments are met on both sides. The specific package is a conversation, but these are the levers we can offer:

Talk to us

Sponsorship and recruiting inquiries go to our VP of Careers, who will send the current sponsorship document.

Start a conversation

Energy Career Fair

A table at our flagship recruiting event each early fall, in front of students attending specifically because they want to work in energy.

Info sessions

A dedicated recruiting event where your team explains what the work actually is, what you look for, and how your timeline runs. We handle logistics, promotion, and turnout.

Speaker events & panels

A lower-pressure format for building name recognition early, often co-hosted with other Penn organizations to broaden the room.

Case competition

Sponsor our end-of-year competition, supply the prompt, or send judges. You see how students think under a real deadline — a better signal than a résumé screen.

Consulting engagements

Bring a scoped problem to our Consulting committee and get a student team's analysis, with a VP accountable for the engagement.

Newsletter & channels

Placement in the monthly newsletter and across our social channels, plus promotion of your application deadlines to the listserv.

Alumni

If you were in WUEG

We maintain an alumni database and work to keep those relationships live. The most useful things a graduate can do, in rough order of effort:

  • Let us add you to the database so current members can find you
  • Take a coffee chat with a member interested in your corner of the sector
  • Speak at a GBM or sit on a panel
  • Flag openings on your team, or bring your firm to the career fair
  • Judge the case competition

Our constitution also provides for advisors — former board members who had a large impact on the club and stay on to give the current board context on past decisions.

Reconnect with WUEG

What we need most

Honestly: warm introductions and time. A thirty-minute call with a member who is trying to understand what a development analyst does is worth more to them than almost anything else we can arrange.

For recruiters in a hurry

If you have a deadline coming up and just want it in front of energy-interested Penn undergraduates, email us. We can put it in the newsletter and on the listserv without a formal sponsorship.
